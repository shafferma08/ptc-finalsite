For Employers

Connect with skilled PTC students and graduates. Our students train in high-demand fields at both PTC campuses, and your job posting on our free job board will reach them all.

Post a Job Opportunity

Free for Employers

Post your opening on Career Rocket®

There's no cost to post your job opening at Pinellas Technical College. Your position goes on Career Rocket®, our job board built exclusively for PTC students and graduates. You'll find hands-on trained professionals ready to hit the ground running.

Once your account is set up, you can post your own positions, job descriptions, required skills, salary info, and more.

Partner with Us: PTC Works!

For decades, Pinellas Technical College has been the community's premier provider of career and technical education. Our network of business and industry partners plays an integral role in ensuring that our students are well-prepared for the ever-changing demands of the workforce. That commitment takes many forms, including offering scholarships, assisting with mock interviews, providing employment opportunities, attending and sponsoring events, and actively serving on our advisory boards.

Each of these organizations comprises the "PTC Works" initiative. We are grateful to each of them for their acknowledgment and support in knowing that PTC Works.
