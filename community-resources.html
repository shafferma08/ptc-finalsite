Quick contacts

Need help right now?

Call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, or dial 211 to reach 211 Tampa Bay Cares for local help in Pinellas County (24 hours a day, 7 days a week).

At PTC?

Students with documented disabilities can request classroom accommodations through PTC Student Services on either campus. Learn about accommodations.

In Pinellas K-12?

Reach the Pinellas County Schools ESE Department at 727-588-6032 or visit pcsb.org.

Before you go
