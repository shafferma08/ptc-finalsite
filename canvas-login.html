Canvas Login

Canvas is where you access your course content, assignments, grades, and announcements. Choose the correct Canvas pathway for your program below.

Choose your Canvas pathway

PCSB Canvas has three sign-in pathways. The pathway you use determines which credentials work, so pick the one that matches your program.

Pathways matter

The Canvas link you use determines which login credentials will work. If you use the wrong pathway (for example, a PTC student using the staff link), your login will fail even if your password is correct.

Make sure you launch the pathway that matches your program below.

PTC Career Students

For standard postsecondary certificate and career program students.

Sign in with your myptc.edu email

Launch Canvas (opens in new tab)

Staff & Dual Enrollment

For instructors and dual enrollment students.

Sign in with your pcsb.org email

Short Course & Apprentice

Looking for Focus?

Canvas is your classroom. Focus is the district Student Information System (SIS), where you check grades, attendance, your schedule, and account balances.

Focus / SIS Portal

Grades, attendance, schedule, and account balances in the Pinellas County Schools Student Information System.

Open Focus (opens in new tab)

Need login help?

If a pathway is not working or you are not sure which one is yours, your counselor or the front office can point you to the right login.
