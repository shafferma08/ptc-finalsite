Automotive Electrical/Electronics/Hybrid-EV Systems Apprenticeship

What You'll Learn

While in this program, apprentices will be provided the opportunity to learn in the following areas:

  • Basic electrical and electronic 12 volt systems including series and parallel circuits, and wiring diagrams
  • General Electrical System Diagnosis
  • Engine Management Systems diagnosis and repair
  • Battery Diagnosis and Service
  • Starting System Diagnosis & Repair
  • Charging System Diagnosis & Repair
  • Lighting System Diagnosis & Repair
  • Instrument Cluster and Driver Information
  • Systems Diagnosis & Repair
  • Body Electrical System Diagnosis & Repair
  • Hybrid/Electric Diagnosis & Repair

Industry Certification & Resources

Industry Certification

ASE A6 | Electronic/Electrical Systems

This apprenticeship prepares apprentices for the ASE A6 Electronic/Electrical Systems industry certification.
