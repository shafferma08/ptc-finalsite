Welcome to ESOL @ PTC-Clearwater!

Pinellas Technical College offers an award-winning ESOL (English for Speakers of Other Languages) program on our Clearwater campus.

Format Self-paced · Day & Evening
Schedule August - May
Cost per Term $45
Location Clearwater

About the ESOL Program

PTC's ESOL program offers a self-paced learning environment with a staff of experienced instructors.

Day and evening classes are available, and the curriculum is tailored to fit your needs.

Class Schedule, August - May

8:00 a.m. - 1:00 p.m.  Monday - Friday (flexible schedule)
4:00 - 9:00 p.m.  Monday - Thursday (flexible schedule)

Cost

The cost per term is $45 (two terms per year); in addition, a $5 fee for an identification badge will be required of all students, and there may be an additional $5 fee for parking (if applicable).

Intake Testing and Registration for ESOL

Monday & Tuesday 4:00 - 8:00 p.m. and
Wednesday & Thursday 9:00 a.m. - 12:00 p.m. (noon) or by appointment.
