St. Petersburg Tuition & Financial Aid

Tuition rates, fees, scholarships, veterans benefits, and refund policy are the same across both PTC campuses. This page covers the few details specific to the St. Petersburg campus.

For institution-wide tuition rates, fees, FAFSA guidance, federal and state aid, scholarships, veterans benefits, and the refund policy, see the main Tuition & Financial Aid page. The St. Petersburg specifics are below.

FAFSA Federal School Code

When completing the FAFSA for the St. Petersburg campus, use:

013917

Financial Aid Office

All students must complete an Institutional Application Form at the Financial Aid Department before starting school so aid can begin processing.
