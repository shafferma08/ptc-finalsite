Apprenticeship Programs

Earn while you learn. PTC-Clearwater apprenticeship programs combine paid on-the-job training with hands-on classroom instruction, so you can build real skills and a real career at the same time.

PTC-Sponsored

Our Apprenticeship Programs

These programs are directly sponsored by Pinellas Technical College. We handle enrollment, instruction, and employer coordination.

Automotive Electrical/Electronics/Hybrid-EV Systems

Train in modern automotive electrical diagnostics, electronics, and hybrid-EV systems. This apprenticeship prepares you for high-demand careers in a rapidly evolving automotive industry.

Learn More

Facilities Maintenance

Develop the skills needed to maintain and repair commercial and institutional facilities. Covers electrical, plumbing, HVAC basics, and building systems in a hands-on apprenticeship format.

Learn More

Get Started

How Apprenticeships Work

Apprenticeships let you earn a wage while training for a skilled career. Here is what to expect.

1

Apply and Get Hired

You are employed by a sponsor company from day one. You earn a paycheck while attending classes at PTC.

2

Train On the Job

Split your time between hands-on work with your employer and classroom instruction at PTC-Clearwater.

3

Earn Your Credential

Complete the program with industry-recognized credentials, real experience, and zero student debt.
