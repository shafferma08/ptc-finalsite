Resources for working with ESE, ESOL & returning students

Professional development, training, and curated supports for teachers and faculty across PTC programs. The PTC Study Group is actively expanding this section. To suggest a resource, email shafferma@pcsb.org.

Professional development partners #

Agencies that explicitly offer training, consultation, or technical assistance to teachers and faculty in Pinellas County.

FDLRS Gulf Coast

Florida Diagnostic and Learning Resources System. Human Resource Development (PD for teachers), Child Find, Parent Services, and assistive and instructional technology support for educators serving exceptional children in Hernando, Pasco, and Pinellas counties.

pcsb.org

Project 10 Transition Education Network

Technical assistance, training, professional development, and resources to Florida school districts on secondary transition services for students with disabilities. Based at USF St. Petersburg.

project10.info

CARD-USF

Center for Autism and Related Disabilities. Instruction and coaching to families and professionals through a training and assistance model. Free for those serving individuals with autism spectrum disorder or related disabilities.

card-usf.fmhi.usf.edu

SEDNET / Pinellas

Multi-Agency Network for Students with Emotional/Behavioral Disabilities. Cross-agency training for families, educators, and community partners, plus problem-solving support and Response to Intervention guidance.

sednetfl.info

VSA Florida (Very Special Arts)

Professional development on building inclusive learning opportunities in the arts. Statewide, regional, and local workshops for teachers, plus art residency programs that model inclusion strategies and curriculum adaptations.

vsafl.org

Family Network on Disabilities

Statewide network connecting families, professionals, and concerned citizens to information and resources. Useful for teachers who want to point families toward training, mentoring, and advocacy support.

FNDUSA.org

Referral playbooks (coming soon)

"When a student tells me X, who do I call?" Quick reference cards organized by common faculty scenarios. Maps the most common situations to the right agency and the right phone number.
