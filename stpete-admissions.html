Apply to St. Petersburg Campus

Students must be at least 16 years of age and not currently enrolled in high school. Most of our programs start five times per school year: August, October, January, March/April, and June.

Complete placement testing

St. Petersburg testing schedule

Schedule a shadow visit

Some programs have specific days and/or dress codes for shadowing, so please speak with a counselor before coming in. Call (727) 893-2500 to inquire about shadowing your program of interest.

Notify outside funding agency

If using an outside funding agency (Career Source, Voc. Rehab, etc.) provide all paperwork to the agency.

Pay fees to finalize

Complete the online registration form. Pay all applicable fees to finalize your registration.

FAFSA & Financial Aid at St. Petersburg

FAFSA School Code

013917

Use this code on your FAFSA application for Pinellas Technical College St. Petersburg campus.

Applicants can also obtain a paper application from the Financial Aid office.

Contact the Financial Aid office

After receiving notification of eligibility (typically within 3-5 business days), contact the Financial Aid office:

VA financial aid: Joanne Schnell, schnellj@pcsb.org

Pell & other financial aid: Sabrina Mitchell, mitchellsa@pcsb.org

Or visit the school in person.

If you qualify for assistance, please note that financial aid may not cover all associated costs, and students may need to pay out-of-pocket for some fees.

More on FAFSA, financial aid, and payment options.
