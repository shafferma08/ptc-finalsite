For employers & partners

Hiring graduates with barriers, with less risk

For Pinellas County employers interested in hiring PTC graduates and other workers who face barriers to employment. The federal and state programs below provide bonding, tax credits, on-the-job training support, and an introduction service through CareerSource Pinellas.

Why this page exists

PTC graduates often face structural barriers when applying for jobs: a disability, a non-native English background, or a record. Employers often want to hire from this pool but worry about insurance, training cost, or risk. The programs on this page exist exactly to defray those costs. Most are free. None of them require you to be a large employer.

Introductions

CareerSource Pinellas

Pinellas County's workforce board acts as the primary bridge between local employers and job seekers with barriers. Post jobs at no cost, get pre-screened candidates, host on-site recruitment events, and coordinate with the Ticket to Work program for Social Security beneficiaries.

careersourcepinellas.com

Bonding

Federal Bonding Program

Free fidelity bonds for employers hiring at-risk workers, including people with criminal records, who would not normally qualify for commercial bonding. Bonds cover the first six months at no cost to the employer. Administered locally through CareerSource centers.

bonds4jobs.com

Tax credit

Work Opportunity Tax Credit (WOTC)

Federal tax credit for employers who hire from targeted groups facing barriers to employment, including returning citizens (ex-felons), SSI recipients, and Vocational Rehabilitation referrals. Credit ranges from $1,200 to $9,600 per qualifying employee depending on the target group.

dol.gov/agencies/eta/wotc

Training support

Vocational Rehabilitation Employer Services

Florida VR partners with employers to identify candidates, fund on-the-job training, and arrange workplace accommodations. Free consulting to design jobs that work for both the employee and the business.

rehabworks.org

Established partner

Goodwill Industries-Suncoast

Operates employment and training services for people with disabilities and other barriers to work, including returning citizens. Can supply job-ready candidates and on-site coaching for new hires. A useful introduction if you don't know where to start.

goodwill-suncoast.org
