# PTC Website Review - Consolidated Report
**Date:** 2026-06-11
**Pages Reviewed:** programs.html, practical-nursing-clearwater.html, welding-stpete.html, apprenticeships-clearwater.html, adult-education-pathways.html, workforce-continuing-education.html, clearwater-contact.html
**Agents:** Prospective Student, Current Student, Parent/Guardian, Faculty, Director, Designer, Accessibility, Finalsite CMS
**Scope:** Scoped review of the new Programs cluster (Waves 0-7) plus new hubs/nav.

## Executive Summary

This is the strongest cluster the panel has reviewed. The new program-page template (Practical Nursing, Welding) finally delivers what every persona has asked for since April: program codes, hours, full course sequences, requirements, credentials, named counselors, real PDFs, and working `apply.myptc.edu` CTAs. That materially advances prior critical/high items C1 (program details), H1 (detail pages exist), and H5 (named counselors), and partly advances C2/C7 (apply path now real on most pages), H4 (welding salary), and H6 (nursing cost sheet). The two newest hubs (apprenticeships, adult-ed) also prove the team can build correctly on the canonical `.card` system.

The biggest risks are accuracy and finish, not concept. Three findings would embarrass PTC in front of a COE visitor or the PCSB board: a Practical Nursing counselor (Merritt Scott) that internal records tie to Phlebotomy, a welding Day schedule the program's own source notes say does not exist (evening-only per the 2026-27 PDF), and a salary-sourcing standard that is applied on welding but deliberately omitted on nursing. A separate hard launch-blocker also surfaced: `adult-education-pathways.html` ships with no `script.js`, so its mobile navigation is completely non-operable, a clean WCAG 2.1.1 failure on a public-institution page. Under the hood, the program pages regressed M5/M6 (one-off card systems and ~400 lines of duplicated per-page CSS), reintroduced the em-dash `<title>` that M14 fixed, and the open H8 Composer-filter architecture question is unresolved.

## What's Working Well (Do Not Change)

- **The program-page template is the win of the cycle.** Practical Nursing and Welding carry program codes, hours, full course sequences with per-course hour badges, requirements, credentials, named counselors, info-session details, and a hero stat bar that answers "how long / what format / where" in two seconds. Every persona called this a major step up. This is the model for all remaining program pages.
- **Trust signals land.** COE/Cognia/PCSB footer block on every page; nursing's Florida State Board approval + HCA Florida Northside partner logo; welding's AWS accredited testing facility; "since 1962." The skeptical-parent persona moved toward "yes" on these.
- **The two newest hubs are canonical-clean.** apprenticeships-clearwater.html and adult-education-pathways.html ride `.card card--accent card--lift` + `.card-grid` with minimal page-local CSS. They are the design + CMS reference pages and prove convergence is achievable.
- **Apply path is real on most pages.** Nursing and campus headers route to `apply.myptc.edu`; the "New to PTC? Start Here" strip (Review Requirements -> Apply Online -> Meet Your Counselor) is genuinely helpful to a first-gen prospect.
- **Apprenticeships messaging.** "Earn while you learn," "zero student debt," and the 3-step "How Apprenticeships Work" are the single most persuasive copy in the set for both prospect and parent.
- **Honest disclosures.** Workforce page states short courses do NOT grant program credit; "no sessions currently scheduled" pills instead of hidden dead courses; TEAS-only-at-PTC warning on nursing. COE-friendly candor.
- **Accessibility base holds.** Skip link (C6), `:focus-visible` (H10), breadcrumb semantics (H13), native `<details>` accordions, labeled filter selects, exemplary video-card aria-labels with "(opens in new tab)," meaningful hero alt text, prefers-reduced-motion. Keep all of it.
- **Campus addresses/phones are consistent and correct** across pages, footers, and utility bars. Two-campus chrome discipline (separate utility bars, headers, footers, schedule targets) is handled correctly.
