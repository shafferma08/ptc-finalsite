# Current Student Review - 2026-06-11

Reviewer persona: Maria, 28, Medical Assisting program, Clearwater campus, 6 months in. Daily user. I come to the site to get to Canvas, check my schedule, find student services hours, and look up campus contact info. I am on my phone most of the time and I am impatient.

Pages reviewed: programs.html, practical-nursing-clearwater.html, welding-stpete.html, apprenticeships-clearwater.html, adult-education-pathways.html, workforce-continuing-education.html, clearwater-contact.html.

## Portal Access

Good news first: Canvas and Focus are now genuinely one click from everywhere I land. On every campus-chrome page (practical-nursing-clearwater, welding-stpete, apprenticeships-clearwater) the campus utility bar has a "Canvas Login" and "SIS Portal / Focus" link wired to the real URLs (myptc.edu/student-links/canvas-login and pinellas.focusschoolsoftware.com/focus). That is exactly what I need and it is the single biggest daily win. The "Current Students" dropdown on those pages repeats Canvas Login, which is fine.

The main-site pages are weaker. On programs.html, adult-education-pathways.html, and workforce-continuing-education.html the utility bar drops the dedicated Canvas link and instead gives me one generic "Student Portal" link to myptc.edu/student-links. That is a landing page, not Canvas. From a phone, that is one extra tap and one extra decision every time. The main-site "Current Students" dropdown does carry a real "Canvas Login" link, but the dropdown is a hover/tap-to-open menu, so the fastest path (the always-visible utility bar) is the slower one on these pages. Inconsistent: campus pages get a direct Canvas link in the bar, main-site pages get a vague "Student Portal."

No Webmail link anywhere. I check student email too. There is Canvas and Focus, but no webmail/student email link in any utility bar or Current Students dropdown on any of these seven pages.

## Finding My Schedule

This is solid on campus pages. Every Clearwater-chrome page has "Class Schedule" as the FIRST item in the Programs dropdown AND the first item in the Current Students dropdown, both pointing to schedule-clearwater.html (titled "2026-2027 Class Schedule - PTC Clearwater"). The welding-stpete page correctly points its schedule links to schedule-stpete.html. So a current student on a campus page can reach the right campus schedule in one tap. The clearwater-contact.html page even has a dedicated "Class Schedule" card in the body. Good.

Two friction points:
- On the main-site pages (programs.html, adult-education-pathways.html), the Current Students dropdown lists BOTH "Clearwater Schedule" and "St. Pete Schedule." As a Clearwater student I have to read and pick every time. The campus-chrome pages solve this by showing only my campus schedule, so the main-site behavior feels like a step back.
- The Current Students nav LABEL on campus pages links the top-level item to schedule-clearwater.html (the parent link is the schedule). That is actually convenient for me, but it is a little odd that "Current Students" the heading equals "Class Schedule." Minor.

## Student Services

Mixed. On campus pages the Current Students dropdown has "Student Services Hours" (clearwater-student-services.html / stpete-student-services.html). That page exists and covers Student Services, Financial Aid, Bookstore, and Records HOURS. Useful for "when is the office open."

But it is hours only. As a current student I am looking for tutoring, academic advising, and career services, and I do not see them surfaced anywhere in the nav on any of these pages. "Student Services Hours" tells me when a counter is open, not where to get tutoring or who helps me with a job after I finish. The program pages route everything to a single program "counselor" (e.g., practical-nursing points to Merritt Scott), which is admissions-flavored, not current-student support. There is no "tutoring," "advising," or "career services" word anywhere I looked.

The bookstore link (bncvirtual.com/ptc) is in the Current Students dropdown on campus pages. Good, that is a real daily-ish need.

## Campus Information

clearwater-contact.html is genuinely good for my needs. Address, phone (with tel: link), fax, "Get directions," a class-schedule card, a staff-directory card, and a map callout. The utility bar also shows the phone and the address. This page answers "how do I reach campus / where is it."

The gap that matters to me: NO HOURS. The contact page promises in its meta description "address, phone, fax, directions, parking, and accessibility" but the actual page has no campus hours and no parking or accessibility detail in the body (those are punted to campus-maps.html). As a current student the question I actually have is "is the building open right now / what time does it close," and this page does not answer it. Student Services HOURS live on a different page (clearwater-student-services.html) that is not linked from the contact page. So campus hours are split across two pages and neither is complete.

## Page-by-Page Notes

### programs.html (main-site chrome)
- What works: Clean A-Z directory, campus + cluster filters work, Current Students dropdown has a real Canvas Login. The Programs > Explore dropdown is a nice addition (All Programs, Workforce & Continuing Education, Apprenticeships, Dual Enrollment, Distance Learning, ABE/GED/ESOL, Student Orgs).
- What needs work: (1) Utility bar uses vague "Student Portal" instead of a direct Canvas link, unlike campus pages. (2) The new top-level "Apprenticeships" nav item points to apprenticeships-workforce.html, but the Explore dropdown's "Apprenticeships" points to apprenticeships-clearwater.html, and the page-body callout offers separate "Clearwater Apprenticeships" / "St. Petersburg Apprenticeships" buttons. Three different apprenticeship destinations from one page is confusing. (3) Explore dropdown lists "Dual Enrollment," "Distance Learning," and "ABE / GED / ESOL" as three separate items that ALL point to the same adult-education-pathways.html. Tapping three different labels and landing on the same page makes me think the menu is broken.
- Suggested fix: Make the utility bar Canvas link match the campus pattern (direct Canvas + Focus). Pick ONE apprenticeships destination and use it consistently in the nav. For the three pathways links, either point them to anchors on adult-education-pathways.html (#dual-enrollment, #distance-learning, #abe-ged-esol) or collapse them into one "Adult Education & Pathways" item.

### practical-nursing-clearwater.html
- What works: Campus utility bar with direct Canvas + Focus. Current Students dropdown complete (schedule, Canvas, services hours, bookstore, records). As a health-program student this is the closest analog to my own MA program page and the daily-access chrome is right.
- What needs work: This is a prospect-focused page (admission requirements, "New to PTC? Start Here," Apply Online, TEAS, info sessions). That is appropriate for the page's job, but nothing on it speaks to an already-enrolled student. The "counselor" block is admissions framing. Not a defect for this page type, just noting the whole experience tilts toward prospects.
- Suggested fix: None required for this page. The chrome already gives enrolled students their daily links.

### welding-stpete.html
- What works: Correctly campus-scoped to St. Pete: schedule links go to schedule-stpete.html, services to stpete-student-services.html, records to stpete-records-request.html. Canvas/Focus direct in the utility bar. Good two-campus discipline.
- What needs work: Same prospect tilt as the nursing page (fine for a program page). The "Apprenticeships" top-nav item again points to apprenticeships-workforce.html, while the rest of the apprenticeship ecosystem points campus-specific. Consistency issue, not unique to this page.
- Suggested fix: Resolve the apprenticeships destination sitewide (see programs.html note).

### apprenticeships-clearwater.html
- What works: Clear two-tier layout (PTC-sponsored vs community), campus chrome with direct Canvas/Focus, schedule and services reachable. As a current student this is easy to skim.
- What needs work: This page is reached from the Programs > Explore dropdown and the programs.html callout, but the actual top-level "Apprenticeships" nav item on this very page points to apprenticeships-workforce.html, NOT to itself or to a campus apprenticeships hub. So clicking the main "Apprenticeships" nav from the Clearwater apprenticeships page takes me to a different (combined) page. Disorienting.
- Suggested fix: Decide whether "Apprenticeships" is one combined hub (apprenticeships-workforce.html) or campus-split. If combined is canonical, route the Explore dropdown and the programs.html callout there too. If campus-split, the top-nav item should be campus-aware on campus pages.

### adult-education-pathways.html
- What works: Real content hub for dual enrollment, distance learning, ABE/GED/ESOL, student orgs. Main-site Current Students dropdown has Canvas Login.
- What needs work: Utility bar "Student Portal" (vague, not Canvas). It is the single destination for three different Explore dropdown labels (see programs.html note), which makes those menu items feel redundant.
- Suggested fix: Add in-page anchors so the three dropdown labels land on the relevant section, and align the utility bar to a direct Canvas link.

### workforce-continuing-education.html
- What works: Main-site Current Students dropdown has Canvas. Content is well organized.
- What needs work: Same vague "Student Portal" utility-bar link. Not really a current-student daily page, so lower stakes for me.
- Suggested fix: Align utility bar Canvas link with campus pattern.

### clearwater-contact.html
- What works: Best contact experience of the set. Address, clickable phone, fax, directions, schedule card, staff directory card, map callout. Campus utility bar has direct Canvas/Focus.
- What needs work: (1) No campus hours on the page despite the meta description promising them, and no parking/accessibility detail in the body. (2) The utility-bar search button is href="#" (dead). (3) The "Schedule a Visit" card is aimed at prospects ("before you apply"); as an enrolled student I would rather this card surface something like office hours or how to reach my own program area.
- Suggested fix: Add a "Campus Hours" block (or pull in the Student Services hours and link to clearwater-student-services.html). Wire the search button. Consider a small "Already a student?" line linking to schedule + services hours.

## Top 3 Issues (ranked by daily inconvenience)

1. Portal access is inconsistent between campus pages and main-site pages, and there is no webmail. Campus pages give me a direct Canvas link in the always-visible utility bar; main-site pages (programs, adult-education-pathways, workforce) replace it with a vague "Student Portal" link to a landing page. Every visit to a main-site page costs me extra taps to get to Canvas. And no page gives me student email. Fix: standardize the utility bar to direct Canvas + Focus everywhere, and add a Webmail link.

2. The "Apprenticeships" navigation is wired to three different destinations. The top-level nav item goes to apprenticeships-workforce.html; the Programs > Explore dropdown goes to apprenticeships-clearwater.html; the programs.html body offers separate Clearwater/St. Pete buttons. Clicking "Apprenticeships" from the Clearwater apprenticeships page sends me to a different page. Pick one canonical destination and apply it consistently. (This is the new nav work and it is the most visibly broken thing.)

3. Student services and campus hours are incomplete and scattered. Tutoring, academic advising, and career services are not surfaced anywhere in the nav. "Student Services Hours" is hours-only and lives on a page that the contact page does not even link to, so campus hours are split across two pages with neither complete. Fix: add a real current-student services section (tutoring / advising / career services) and put campus + office hours on the contact page.
