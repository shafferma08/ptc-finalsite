# Parent/Guardian Review - 2026-06-15

Reviewer persona: Sandra, 45, mother of a 17-year-old in Pinellas County interested in automotive technology. I am skeptical of anything that isn't a "real college," I am helping pay, and I am comparing PTC against St. Petersburg College and other career schools. I need to feel reassured this is safe, reputable, and worth the money before I let my son commit.

Pages reviewed: index.html, clearwater.html, stpete.html, programs.html, admissions.html, tuition-aid.html, practical-nursing-clearwater.html, contact.html, consumer-information.html, about.html.

---

## Trust & Credibility

My first impression is genuinely reassuring, and that matters. This does not look like a diploma mill. It looks like a real institution.

What earns my trust on the very first screen:
- The footer on **every** page carries three accreditation marks with plain-language captions: "Accredited by the Council on Occupational Education," "Accredited as part of Pinellas County Schools" (Cognia), and "Part of Pinellas County Schools." That last one is the single most reassuring thing on the site for a skeptical parent. PTC is not some pop-up storefront. It is run by the same school board that runs my son's high school.
- The homepage "Why Choose PTC" section leads with a "COE Accredited" badge and the line "Both campuses individually accredited by the Council on Occupational Education." It also says "For over 60 years" and shows an "Est. 1962" badge. A 60-year track record is exactly what I am looking for.
- Accreditation is easy to find. It is in the top nav under "About PTC > Accreditation," in the footer of every page, on about.html, and on consumer-information.html, where I get the actual COE address (7840 Roswell Road, Atlanta) and phone (770-396-3898) plus Cognia's. Being able to call the accreditor myself, without asking PTC, is a real trust signal. A diploma mill would never hand me that.
- The "By the Numbers" band on about.html (60+ programs, 40+ career areas, 5,000+ full-time students, 250+ industry partners) plus a linked "Annual Impact Report (PDF)" reads like a public institution that reports to taxpayers.

Where my skepticism flares:
- **No outcome data tied to those trust claims.** "Graduates don't just earn certificates. They launch careers." is asserted everywhere but never backed with a single number. As a paying parent, "industry-certified" and "job-ready" are marketing words until I see a job-placement rate or a graduate count.
- The Practical Nursing page (which is the closest thing to a finished program page) states **"Top Ranked LPN Program"** in a trust strip and again in the credentials section, sourced to PracticalNursing.org. That is good, but it is the *only* program with that kind of credibility badge, which makes me wonder why automotive (my son's interest) has nothing comparable.
- The leadership cards on about.html still use **initials in circles (JP / JS / DH)** instead of actual photos. Real people with real faces would reassure me far more than monogram placeholders, which read as "we haven't finished this."

Overall: credibility is the site's strongest dimension. The accreditation and PCSB governance story is clear, repeated, and verifiable. That alone moves PTC from "career school I'm suspicious of" to "real school I'll consider."

## Cost & Value

This is where a paying parent lives, and it is a mixed picture.

What works well:
- **tuition-aid.html is excellent and honest.** It shows an actual tuition table: Career Technical Certificate at **$2.92 per hour** (resident) / $11.71 (nonresident), set by the Pinellas County School Board. That per-hour rate, against a community college, is a genuinely strong value argument, and seeing a real number (not "contact us") builds enormous trust.
- It lists the real fees I will actually be hit with (application, ID, parking, books/tools, lab, equipment, graduation, uniforms, exam fees). No surprises buried later. The honest line "All students must come to the bookstore to pay fees each term; payment does not happen automatically" is the kind of practical detail that tells me this was written by people who actually run the place.
- Financial aid is thorough and credible: FAFSA codes per campus (005605 Clearwater, 013917 St. Petersburg), Pell, Florida Bright Futures, the Career Education Student Assistance Grant, Vocational Rehabilitation, Youth Connect, CareerSource. A four-step "How Financial Aid Works" walkthrough. A real **Refund Policy** section (45-day refunds, first-10-sessions window, nonrefundable fees spelled out). A Net Price Calculator per campus. Veterans benefits in real depth.
- Nine named **scholarships** with deadlines and links, including an automotive-adjacent one (AFCEA for Computer Systems, Electronics, etc.) and several open to high schoolers. As a parent funding this, a real scholarship list is gold.
- The homepage promises "Financial aid, scholarships, and payment plans available to all students," and tuition-aid.html largely delivers (though I never actually find a "payment plan" explained anywhere; see issues).

Where the value case breaks down:
- **There is no tuition total per program, and no cost on any program page except a PDF.** The per-hour rate is great, but I cannot do the multiplication myself unless I know the program's hours. The Practical Nursing page does it right: it shows "1,350 Hours" and links a real "Program Costs" PDF. But for automotive, I cannot yet see what 1,350 or 1,800 hours actually costs me out the door. I am told this is a known live-gated gap (H6), but for an enrollment decision it is the gap.
- **ROI / payoff messaging is entirely absent.** Nowhere does the site tell me what a PTC automotive grad earns, what a Tampa Bay starting wage looks like, or how fast the program pays for itself versus four years of college debt. The Practical Nursing source comment even says salary data was "Omitted (no live source)." For a parent weighing this against a "real college," the unanswered question "will this actually get my kid a paycheck, and how big?" is the whole ballgame. The site sells affordability but never closes the loop on return.
- **The value-vs-4-year-college argument is never made explicitly.** The site says PTC is affordable, hands-on, and fast. It never says, in words a skeptical parent can quote back to herself, "Here is why a 1,350-hour certificate that costs $X and lands a job beats two years of community college." I am left to assemble that case myself.

Net: I can understand the *cost* well (strong). I cannot understand the *value/return* at all (weak). For my decision those are two different questions and only one is answered.

## Safety & Campus Life

For a parent of a 17-year-old who will be on a campus with adult students, safety is not optional, and PTC handles it better than I expected from a "career school," with one real gap.

What works:
- consumer-information.html has a dedicated **"Campus Safety & Security Data"** section linking each campus's annual Safety & Security Data reports. I can read the actual crime/safety data per campus. That is a serious, institution-grade disclosure.
- A **"Sexual Misconduct & Sexual Predators"** section names exactly who to call (the campus director, with both phone numbers) and gives the FDLE 1-888-FL-PREDATOR hotline and website so I can check the registry around either campus myself. For a parent of a minor, this is precisely the information I want and rarely find.
- A full **Non-Discrimination & Title IX** statement with a named Compliance Office (727-588-6285) and a **Section 504 / ADA Coordinator** (Stephanie Miller, 727-588-6296). Accommodations are addressed on admissions.html too.
- A **Code of Conduct** section (St. Petersburg has a current 2025-26 PDF).

Where it falls short for a parent:
- The Clearwater **Code of Conduct is still "Pending."** My son is interested in automotive, which is offered at Clearwater. The behavior-and-discipline rules for the exact campus he would attend are not posted; I am told to "refer to PCSB district policies in the meantime." That is the one campus where I most need this, and it is the one that is blank.
- **Campus hours, parking, and what daily life looks like are hard to find.** The homepage and campus pages do not show campus hours. contact.html gives me phone and fax but no office hours and no general email. As a parent, "what time does the campus open, where does my kid park, who do I call if he's sick" are basic questions the public pages don't answer in one place.
- **There is no "Parents/Families" section anywhere.** Safety info exists, but it is filed under "Consumer Information," a label a worried parent would not think to click. Nothing on the site speaks to me as a parent specifically.

Net: the safety *substance* is strong and genuinely reassuring. The *findability* for a parent (buried under Consumer Information) and the missing Clearwater Code of Conduct are the weak points.

## Outcomes

This is the weakest area of the site and the one most likely to stall my decision.

- **No job placement rate. Anywhere.** Not on the homepage, not on programs.html, not on the Practical Nursing page, not on about.html. The "250+ Industry Partners" stat and the "Industry Partnerships: Direct connections to Tampa Bay employers for internships and job placement" claim imply outcomes without ever stating one.
- **No NCLEX/credential pass rates** even on the flagship Practical Nursing page, which is exactly where a parent expects them.
- **No salary or wage data** for any program. For my son's automotive interest, I cannot find what an entry-level auto tech earns locally, nor what PTC grads specifically earn.
- **No graduate success stories or testimonials.** The closest thing is the Practical Nursing "See It In Action" videos (students and instructors) and the "Top Ranked LPN Program" badge plus the HCA Florida Northside Hospital clinical partner logo, which are genuinely good and exactly the kind of proof I want. Automotive has none of this.
- The employer-facing copy ("PTC graduates are job-ready, industry-certified, and eager to work") tells employers my kid is ready, but never tells *me, the parent paying for it,* that graduates actually got hired.

I understand from the brief that placement rates, NCLEX pass rates, and salary are known live-gated gaps (H4/H6). I am flagging them anyway because, from my chair, outcomes are the deciding factor. A reputable-looking school with zero published outcomes still leaves me nervous about writing the check.

## Page-by-Page Notes

### index.html (homepage)
- **What works:** Strong, professional first impression. Accreditation badge and "Est. 1962" up high. Clear two-campus story. "Affordable Tuition" and "Industry Partnerships" feature cards. Footer accreditation marks. Quick links include Tuition & Aid, Visit Campus, Contact Us, all the things a parent reaches for.
- **What needs work:** The hero stats ("40+ Career Programs, 250+ Industry Partners, 2 Campus Locations") are activity metrics, not outcome metrics. No placement or salary number. "They launch careers" is asserted with no proof. Transportation & Logistics card names "Automotive Service, Collision Repair, Diesel Technology, and Marine," which is reassuring for my son, but the card just routes to a filtered list, not a confidence-building program overview.
- **Suggested fix:** Add one outcome stat to the hero or Why-PTC band once live data exists (e.g., "X% of graduates employed or continuing education"). Until then, add a single proof point such as a graduate count or "X credentials earned in 2025."

### clearwater.html
- **What works:** Clean campus landing. The Featured Programs cards now name real, specific programs. Transportation card reads "Automotive Service Technology 1 & 2, Diesel Systems Technician, and Marine Service Technologies," and Skilled Trades reads "Cabinetmaking, Electricity, HVAC/R 1, Machining Technologies, and Advanced Welding Technology." These are concrete and believable, no phantom programs spotted. (Verifies the noted content fix.)
- **What needs work:** No campus hours, no parking info, no "what to expect on a visit." The "+12 More" style tags from the homepage don't carry the daily-logistics info a parent wants. News section is the same three items shown on St. Pete (generic).
- **Suggested fix:** Add a short "Visiting Clearwater" block with campus hours and parking, or link it prominently. This is the campus my automotive-interested son would attend, so it carries extra weight.

### stpete.html
- **What works:** Same clean template. Featured Programs name real programs per campus (Health: "Dental Assisting, Emergency Medical Technician, Surgical Technology, Medical Coder/Biller"; Transportation: "Commercial Vehicle Driving (CDL), Class B Driving, Automotive Service, and Collision Repair"). No phantom programs spotted.
- **What needs work:** Same missing campus hours/parking and same generic news items as Clearwater.
- **Suggested fix:** Same as Clearwater. Also make the two campuses' news genuinely campus-specific so each page feels real and current.

### programs.html
- **What works:** Real automotive program pages exist and are named properly: "Automotive Collision Technology," "Master Automotive Service Technology 1" and "2" with Clearwater and St. Petersburg links. As the parent of an automotive kid, seeing dedicated, campus-specific automotive pages is exactly what I want.
- **What needs work:** The A-Z cards show no program length, no credential, and no cost, so I cannot comparison-shop automotive against, say, an SPC program without clicking into each one. No outcomes on the listing.
- **Suggested fix:** Add a one-line "length + credential" under each program card (the data already exists on detail pages). It lets a parent scan and compare without 20 clicks.

### admissions.html
- **What works:** Genuinely parent-friendly. Clear "Students must be at least 16 and not currently enrolled in high school," which directly answers whether my 17-year-old qualifies. Honest 7-step checklist (apply, talk to a counselor, financial aid, placement testing, shadow visit, pay fees, notify funding agency). A real "Schedule a shadow visit" card, exactly the kind of try-before-you-buy step that reassures a skeptical parent. Accommodations section. Counselor links.
- **What needs work:** Application timeline/turnaround is vague ("Most programs start five times per year" but no "apply by X to start in August"). Campus visits route off to per-campus pages rather than giving me a date or a "request a tour" action right here.
- **Suggested fix:** Add an expected processing timeline and a single, obvious "Request a tour / shadow visit" action on this page so a parent can act immediately.

### tuition-aid.html
- **What works:** The strongest page on the site for me. Real tuition rates, real fees, real refund policy, deep financial aid and veterans content, nine scholarships with deadlines, FAFSA codes, Net Price Calculator. This page alone makes PTC feel honest about money.
- **What needs work:** "Payment plans" are promised on the homepage but I never find one explained here. No per-program total cost (only per-hour). No connection between cost and earnings (ROI).
- **Suggested fix:** Either add a short "Payment Plans" subsection or stop promising them on the homepage. Add a "typical total cost by program" pointer (e.g., "see each program page for total hours x rate") so the per-hour figure becomes a real dollar amount in a parent's head.

### practical-nursing-clearwater.html
- **What works:** This is the model every program page should follow, and it shows me what a *finished* automotive page could look like. It has hours (1,350), duration (~15 months), credential (LPN & CNA), "Approved by the Florida State Board of Nursing," a "Top Ranked LPN Program" badge, a real clinical partner (HCA Florida Northside Hospital), student videos, a course sequence, a named counselor with email and phone, a Program Costs PDF, and articulation agreements toward an RN. If automotive had this, my skepticism would mostly evaporate.
- **What needs work:** Even here, no NCLEX-PN pass rate and no salary (the source note says salary was omitted). "Upcoming Classes" shows "TBD / TBD," which reads as "no classes coming." The named counselor (Merritt Scott) is flagged internally as possibly misattributed (tracker C9), so a parent could email the wrong person.
- **Suggested fix:** Add the pass rate and a sourced wage range when available. Replace "TBD / TBD" with a real next start date or "Contact your counselor for the next start date." Verify the counselor before this is shown to families.

### contact.html
- **What works:** Both campuses with address, main phone, fax, directions links, and a per-campus "Contact & Visit Info" button. Easy to reach a human.
- **What needs work:** No campus/office hours, no general admissions email, and the page-level contact form referenced in the CSS does not actually appear in the campus-card layout I see. A parent with a quick question has to call; some of us would rather email or fill a form.
- **Suggested fix:** Add office hours and a general inquiry email or a working "Ask Admissions a Question" form. Parents often research at night (like me) and want to send a question, not call during the day.

### consumer-information.html
- **What works:** This is the trust backbone of the site: accreditation with accreditor contacts, non-discrimination, accessibility, campus safety data, sexual misconduct/predator info, financial reports, code of conduct, records requests, compliance contacts. Comprehensive and credible.
- **What needs work:** It is buried under "About PTC" and titled "Consumer Information," which no parent thinks to click when they're worried about safety. The Clearwater Code of Conduct is still "Pending." Some financial reports are dated (FY2017-2020).
- **Suggested fix:** Surface the safety content under a parent-obvious label (a "Safety" or "For Families" entry point) and resolve the Clearwater Code of Conduct, since Clearwater is where automotive is taught.

### about.html
- **What works:** Tells the institutional story well: since 1962, 5,000+ students, 250+ industry partners, part of Pinellas County Schools, mission/vision/core values (including "Maintain a safe environment conducive to learning"), accreditation, named leadership, Annual Impact Report. This is the page that convinces me PTC is a real, established institution.
- **What needs work:** Leadership cards use JP/JS/DH monogram placeholders instead of photos. No outcomes anywhere. The value-vs-4-year-college case is implied, never stated.
- **Suggested fix:** Add real leadership photos. Add a short, plain-language "Why a technical college" value paragraph aimed at families weighing PTC against a traditional college.

## Top 3 Issues (ranked by impact on trust and enrollment decision)

1. **No published outcomes anywhere: job placement, credential pass rates, or graduate wages (tracker H4/H6).** This is the single biggest barrier to my saying yes. The site looks reputable and proves it is accredited, but it never proves it *works*. For a parent helping pay and comparing against a "real college," "will my kid get a job, and earning what?" is the deciding question, and it is unanswered on every page including the flagship Practical Nursing page. *Suggested fix:* publish at least one credible outcome metric per program (or institution-wide) and one sourced local wage range, starting with high-interest programs like automotive and nursing. Even a single honest number ("X% of 2025 graduates employed in field") would change my decision. Set one sourcing rule sitewide so one program isn't allowed to cite a wage while a sister program omits it.

2. **Cost is clear but program-level total cost and any ROI framing are missing.** tuition-aid.html earns real trust with a $2.92/hour rate, real fees, and a refund policy, but I cannot turn that into a total for my son's automotive program without hunting, and nothing tells me what that investment returns. The promised "payment plans" are never explained. *Suggested fix:* show total estimated cost per program (hours x rate, the way the Nursing Costs PDF does), explain or remove the payment-plan promise, and add a brief "what it costs vs. what you can earn" comparison so the affordability story actually closes into a value story.

3. **Safety content is strong but mis-filed for parents, and the Clearwater Code of Conduct is still "Pending."** The safety, predator-registry, and Title IX information is exactly what I need, but it is hidden under "Consumer Information," a label a worried parent would never click, and the discipline rules for the exact campus my son would attend (Clearwater, automotive) are blank. *Suggested fix:* add a parent-obvious entry point to the safety/disclosure content (a "Safety" or "Families" link in the nav or footer), and publish the Clearwater Code of Conduct so both campuses are at parity before this goes in front of families.
