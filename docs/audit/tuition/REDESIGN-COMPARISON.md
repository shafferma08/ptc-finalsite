## Top 5 priority fixes

1. **FAFSA Federal School Code is wrong in 3 places.** Redesign hard-codes **013847**; live publishes **005605 (Clearwater)** and **013917 (St. Petersburg)** as two distinct campus codes. A wrong code misroutes federal aid. Fix the FAFSA card, the FAQ, and the How Aid Works step, and replace the "use the same code for both campuses" claim with the two per-campus codes. (`federal-and-state-funding.md` both campuses.)

2. **Tuition rates are wrong.** Redesign says **$2.91 / $11.64 per clock hour**; live is **$2.92 / $11.71** verbatim on both campus fees pages. Correct both rates and delete the fabricated total-tuition estimates and "900–1,800 hours" range derived from them. Also correct "set by the Florida Legislature" to "established by the Pinellas County School Board." (`fees-and-expenses.md` both campuses.)

3. **Refund policy is fabricated/distorted.** Redesign claims "full refund during the first week of instruction." Live policy is a **10 class sessions / 50 enrollment hours (whichever first)** window for tuition and lab fees, refunds within **45 days**, with active-military options and a nonrefundable fee list. Replace the vague FAQ answer with the verbatim live policy. (`refund-policy.md` both campuses.)

4. **Bill Young Veteran Tuition Waiver and Title 38 provisions are missing.** The Bill Young out-of-state fee waiver (HB 7015 / Ch. 2014-1), the Purple Heart waiver (HB 851 / Ch. 2014-62), the Title 38 USC 3679(e) pending-payment provisions, and the Chapter 35 online restriction appear on multiple live pages and are statutorily encouraged to be prominent. The redesign instead lists fabricated GI Bill chapters (30, 1606), a fabricated MyCAA $4,000 figure, and fabricated Tuition Assistance language. Strip the unsupported veterans claims and add the verbatim live waiver/Title 38 content. (`fees-and-expenses.md`, `veterans-benefits.md` both campuses.)

5. **Adult General Education tuition tier and named scholarships are missing.** The redesign omits the live AGE rates (**$45/term resident, cap $90/yr; $120/term nonresident, cap $360/yr**) entirely, and replaces eight-plus named live scholarships (AWS, AFCEA, Bailey $5,000, MTS $2,000, Passmore, Pinellas Education Foundation, Barrett VA, Nursing Service, FCSUA) with a generic "applications open each fall" line that has no live basis. Add the AGE tuition row verbatim and route the named scholarship list to the IA recommender / follow-ups. (`fees-and-expenses.md`, `scholarships.md` both campuses.)
