## Body (verbatim)

Intro paragraph:
> Pinellas Technical College offers GED and Adult Basic Education at both of its campuses, and we have an award-winning ESOL (English for Speakers of Other Languages) program on our Clearwater campus.

> PTC's adult education programs offer self-paced learning centers with a staff of experienced instructors and the following classes:

Classes list:
- Adult Basic Education (ABE)
- Academic Skills Building (ASB)
- GED Preparation
- English for Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL)  [links to ESOL @ PTC-Clearwater, `/fs/pages/1215`]

**GETTING STARTED** list:
- **Choose a career** from the many programs offered by Pinellas Technical College, OR **get your GED**. See a guidance counselor for assistance.
- Take the CASAS test - make sure to bring a photo ID (required) and dress according to PCSB dress code when you come to test; arrive 30 minutes prior to the scheduled testing time. **For the CASAS test schedule and locations, please click here.** [links to Testing, `/fs/pages/1159`]
- You will receive your test results as soon as you finish testing!
- An individualized plan of study will be created for you, to help you meet your goals - there are both day and evening, self-paced classes that will fit into your busy schedule.
- It's really pretty simple ... **all you need to do is get started!**

Following paragraph:
> Day and evening classes are available, attendance is flexible, and the curriculum is tailored to fit your needs.
