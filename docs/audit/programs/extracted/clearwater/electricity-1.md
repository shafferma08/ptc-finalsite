## Course sequence (verbatim — codes + hours)

1. **Electrician Helper - Course BCV0603 (300 hours)** – Content includes basic safety and code, tools, basic circuits, diagramming, and employability skills.
2. **Residential Electrician - Course BCV0640 (450 hours)** – Students are instructed in electrical math, all phases of residential wiring circuits and installation.
