**Mission Statement:** Students will learn about the digital media production industry while mastering the operations of all aspects of studio and field equipment and workflows.

**Program K100200 consists of a planned sequence of courses.**

**Theory and Foundations of Design (DIG0081)** - 150 hours. Upon completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate knowledge of multimedia/design foundations, basic computer skills, photo and video editing software, digital image composition, camera movements and color modes.

**Multimedia Digital Designer (DIG0082)** - 300 hours. Upon completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate knowledge in fonts, illustration software, design layout software, advanced design processes, presentation software and equipment to produce complex digital video productions.

**Multimedia Integrated Producer Designer (DIG0084)** - 300 hours. Upon completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate knowledge in audio fundamentals, using audio recording/editing software, using studio and field cameras to capture a variety of video shots, studio and field lighting techniques, video editing software and equipment, and become content creators for a variety of screens.

**Multimedia Web/Interactive Designer (DIG0085)** - 150 hours. Upon completion of this course, students will be able to demonstrate knowledge of key production principles, a variety of camera operations, understand HTML and CSS, preliminary webpage design, SEO, CODECS for optimizing playback experiences and platforms, apply job readiness, career planning, and job seeking skills.

No distance learning at this time.

**Industry Certifications**

Students in the program are provided with personal Adobe Creative Cloud accounts and access. They learn from an instructor with a 15 year professional relationship with Adobe as a trainer, mentor and Adobe Education Leader.

Students are equipped and given opportunities to certify in Adobe Creative Cloud products: Premiere, Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects and more. Finish the program with Adobe Certified Professional certifications and a professional portfolio of your productions.
