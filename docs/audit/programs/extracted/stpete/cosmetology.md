## Program statement
Program D500100 consists of a planned sequence of four courses.

## Courses (verbatim, name / code / hours / description)
1. **Grooming and Salon Services, Facials and Nails - Course CSP0009 (225 Hours)** – Content includes demonstrating the practice of performing manicures, pedicures, and apply artificial nails/nail wraps. Perform facials, make-up, hair removal, and artificial lash application.
2. **Cosmetologist and Hairdresser (1 of 3) - Course COS0002 (300 Hours)** – Identify shampoo/hair conditioners and scalp treatments. Identify and perform hair shaping (cutting).
3. **Cosmetologist and Hairdresser, (2 of 3) - Course COS0003 (300 Hours)** – Identify and perform hairstyles. Identify and prepare hairpieces, wigs, and hair attachments.
4. **Cosmetologist and Hairdresser, (3 of 3) - Course COS0009 (375 Hours)** – Identify and perform permanent waving/reconstruction and curl/chemical relaxing. Identify and apply temporary/semi-permanent and permanent color/bleach and specialty color techniques.Completers may be eligible to take the Cosmetology State Examination. Those passing the exam receive the title Cosmetologist and a state license.

## Services Available (verbatim)
All work is performed by Cosmetology students: haircutting, shampoo and set, blow dry/curling iron, press and curl, permanent wave chemical relaxer, hair coloring, highlighting, facials, skin care, tweeze, wax, manicure, and pedicure. **To speak with an instructor, make an appointment, or to discuss costs or details, please call (727) 893-2500, extension 2329.**
