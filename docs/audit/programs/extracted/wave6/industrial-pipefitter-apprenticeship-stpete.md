## Mission / Overview (VERBATIM)
> The mission of this program is to progress employees (working at one of our apprenticeship partners) through a three- to four-year program (depending on the specialty selected) to build a more qualified workforce of employees prepared to construct, install and/or maintain the potable, wastewater, and stormwater systems. This apprenticeship program combines in-classroom theory as well as on-the-job training.

## Length (VERBATIM)
- Meter Repairer – 3 Years
- Water, Wastewater, and Stormwater Pipefitter – 4 Years

## Description / How it works (VERBATIM, four specialty tracks)
- **Meter Repairer Apprenticeship:** This apprenticeship program includes training in small and large water meter repair; backflow assembly testing and repair; reclaimed water inspection for compliance with city and state laws and regulations; and water quality response.
- **Water Pipefitter Apprenticeship:** This apprenticeship program includes training in the installation of new water services, the installation and maintenance of irrigation systems, and the installation of meters, valves, hydrants and backflow devices.
- **Wastewater Pipefitter Apprenticeship:** This apprenticeship program includes training in the construction and repair of sewer pipes and manholes, the investigation of wastewater collection, and cleaning wastewater lines.
- **Stormwater Pipefitter Apprenticeship:** This apprenticeship program includes training in surface maintenance, pipeline accessories, stormwall construction, pavement construction, forestry maintenance, and pipeline construction.
