Continuing Workforce & Life Enrichment Courses

High-quality courses developed by PTC instructors as short Continuing Workforce Development courses. Due to their abbreviated nature, these courses do NOT articulate or grant credit toward PTC's program level courses.

  • Air Conditioning Service Fundamentals

    St. Petersburg Campus

    Gain the skills needed to install and service air conditioning systems.

    Course Details & Register →

  • Child Care Staff or Director Credential Renewal Courses

    St. Petersburg Campus

    DCF-approved course for staff and directors seeking to renew their state administrator credential.
    Download 2025–2026 Child Care Training Program Dates (PDF)

    Course Details & Register →

  • Drone Pilot: Small Unmanned Aircraft Systems

    St. Petersburg Campus

    Prep for your FAA Remote Pilot Certification through this hands-on sUAS training course.

    Course Details & Register →

  • Forklift Operation & Safety

    St. Petersburg Campus

    Earn hands-on forklift operator training in a structured one-day session.

    Course Details & Register →

  • Water Treatment Plant Operation Study Session (Online)

    St. Petersburg Campus

    Online tutorial support for students preparing for water treatment plant operator licensure.

    Course Details & Register →

  • School Board Employee Training

    St. Petersburg Campus

    Training available exclusively to Pinellas County School Board employees.

    View Details →

  • Welding (Evening Class)

    Clearwater Campus

    Evening welding class for adults offered at the PTC Clearwater Campus.

    View Program Details →
