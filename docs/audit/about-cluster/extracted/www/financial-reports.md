Pinellas County Schools - School Financial Reports

School Financial Reports contain comparative data at the school, district and state levels.

Cost as a Percent of Revenue (CAPOR) calculates expenses per FTE program, displaying revenue, cost per FTE, and staffing numbers.

Fiscal Transparency Report offers graphical representations of fiscal trend information for the previous three fiscal years, including metrics like FTE-to-personnel ratios and expenditures per FTE.
