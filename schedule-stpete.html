How to read this schedule

Days: M = Monday, T = Tuesday, W = Wednesday, R = Thursday, F = Friday.

Start Date: The St. Petersburg academic year is divided into five terms. For full-time programs, the numbers (for example, "Terms 1, 3, 5") show which terms you can begin a program. Adult Education start dates are still being finalized. Apprenticeships show either a specific start date or "See program page" if enrollment runs on a separate timeline.

Locations: Most programs meet at the main St. Petersburg campus (901 34th St S). One section of Computer Systems & Information Technology meets at the Clearview Adult Education Center, a separate PTC location. The St. Petersburg Fire and Rescue Apprenticeship meets at St. Pete Fire and Rescue Facilities, and the Practical Nursing Apprenticeship meets at BayCare Facilities.

Apprenticeships: Employment with a participating agency is required before registration on all apprenticeship programs. Contact a counselor for details.

Program lengths and start dates are approximate and subject to change. Contact a counselor to confirm enrollment availability before applying.
