Apprenticeships & Workforce

Earn while you learn through a registered apprenticeship, or build new skills with a short course or evening class. Explore the options below.

What Is an Apprenticeship?

A registered apprenticeship lets you start working in your field right away and get paid while you train. You split your time between hands-on work with an employer and classroom instruction at PTC, and you finish with a credential and real experience.

1

Apply and Get Hired

You are employed by a sponsor company from day one. You earn a paycheck while attending classes at PTC.

2

Train On the Job

Split your time between hands-on work with your employer and classroom instruction at PTC.

3

Earn Your Credential

Complete the program with industry-recognized credentials, real experience, and zero student debt.
