Apprenticeship Programs

Earn while you learn. PTC-St. Petersburg apprenticeship programs combine paid on-the-job training with hands-on classroom instruction, so you can build real skills and a real career at the same time.

St. Petersburg Campus

Apprenticeship Programs

Pinellas Technical College provides the related classroom instruction for these apprenticeships. Apprentices must be employed by or recommended by a participating employer. Contact our admissions team to learn how to get started.

Child Care Apprenticeship

Train for a career in early childhood and child care while you work. This apprenticeship pairs paid on-the-job experience with classroom instruction at PTC-St. Petersburg.

Learn More

Fire Fighting Apprenticeship

Prepare for a career in fire service through paid on-the-job training and classroom instruction at PTC-St. Petersburg. Includes CPAT and FireTEAM testing information.

Learn More

Industrial Machinery Maintenance Apprenticeship

Learn to install, maintain, and repair industrial machinery and equipment. This apprenticeship combines paid work with classroom instruction at PTC-St. Petersburg.

Learn More

Industrial Pipefitter Apprenticeship

Train to lay out, assemble, and maintain piping systems used in industrial settings. Paid on-the-job training is paired with classroom instruction at PTC-St. Petersburg.

Learn More

Plumbing Technology Apprenticeship

Build the skills to install and service plumbing systems. This apprenticeship combines paid on-the-job training with classroom instruction at PTC-St. Petersburg.

Learn More

Roadway Technician Apprenticeship

Train for a career building and maintaining roadways and transportation infrastructure. Paid on-the-job training is paired with classroom instruction at PTC-St. Petersburg.

Get Started

How Apprenticeships Work

Apprenticeships let you earn a wage while training for a skilled career. Here is what to expect.

1

Apply and Get Hired

You are employed by a sponsor company from day one. You earn a paycheck while attending classes at PTC.

2

Train On the Job

Split your time between hands-on work with your employer and classroom instruction at PTC-St. Petersburg.

3

Earn Your Credential

Complete the program with industry-recognized credentials, real experience, and zero student debt.
