Clearwater Tuition & Financial Aid

Tuition rates, fees, scholarships, veterans benefits, and refund policy are the same across both PTC campuses. This page covers the details specific to the Clearwater campus, including our Military & Veteran Student Resources.

For institution-wide tuition rates, fees, FAFSA guidance, federal and state aid, scholarships, veterans benefits, and the refund policy, see the main Tuition & Financial Aid page. The Clearwater specifics are below.

FAFSA Federal School Code

When completing the FAFSA for the Clearwater campus, use:

005605

Net Price Calculator

Estimate your cost after grants and aid with the Clearwater campus calculator.

Open the Clearwater Net Price Calculator

Financial Aid Office

Clearwater Campus: (727) 538-7167, Option 7. After completing the FAFSA, follow up after 3 to 5 days for next steps.

All students must complete an Institutional Application Form at the Financial Aid Department before starting school so aid can begin processing.

Military & Veteran Student Resources

PTC-Clearwater is proud to serve those who serve. The Military & Veteran Resource Center (MVRC) in Building 1 supports active-duty members, veterans, and their families with VA benefits, applying with VA education benefits, events, and a dedicated support team (Lidija Milisav and Susan Welden).
