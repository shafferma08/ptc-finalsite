The printed 2025-26 PTC catalog is the authoritative source for career-cluster and program names (digital file lost; Marianne photographed it 2026-07-06). Full transcription in `docs/audit/catalog-2025-26-taxonomy.md`.

The catalog uses **10 clusters**: Arts & Audio/Visual, Automotive & Transportation, Beauty, Business & Information Technology, Child Care, Construction & Design, Health Science, Hospitality, Manufacturing, Public Works. The redesign's `programs.html` currently uses only 8 differently-named clusters and needs re-bucketing to match.

**Why:** Marianne's manager flagged (2026-07-06) that our cluster names diverge from the catalog and that COE forbids abbreviated program names. The catalog confirms the manager's proposed renames (Skilled Trades → Construction & Design, Cosmetology & Barbering → Beauty).

**How to apply:** Align programs.html cluster filter/nav/chips and per-card data-cluster to the 10 catalog clusters; use full program names verbatim from the catalog. Pending reconciliation includes new programs (Jewelry Design and Repair 1 & 2), renames, and reassignments. See discrepancy list in the taxonomy doc. Don't invent names for the ambiguous items (Television Production Technology vs Digital Media, CDL, Building Trades, Electricity 1). Related: [[coe-full-program-names]].
